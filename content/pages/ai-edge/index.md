Google Ads rules for healthcare and addiction services
Advertising in regulated healthcare verticals requires exact policy knowledge, because generalized assumptions cause preventable disruptions. Platform rules differ sharply between addiction treatment, general mental health counseling, and other clinical services.
Under Google's [Healthcare and medicines](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031) policy, recovery-oriented drug and alcohol addiction services advertising in the United States require LegitScript addiction-services certification plus separate Google certification. Services unrelated to drug and alcohol addiction, including impulse-control disorders, behavioral addiction, and nicotine addiction, sit outside that certification requirement.
Even when LegitScript addiction certification does not apply, health content still operates under strict targeting limits. Under Google's [Health in personalized advertising](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701855) policy, health content such as counseling for depression, anxiety, and addiction falls inside Google's sensitive interest category. In that vertical, advertiser-curated audiences including retargeting, Customer Match, audience expansion, and lookalike segments are not permitted, though predefined Google audiences may still be used.
Policy failures can lead to disapproved ads, limited serving, or account action, depending on the specific policy. Marketing Powered is LegitScript-aware and HIPAA-aware, building acquisition programs around high-intent search structure, compliant messaging, and conversion-focused landing pages rather than restricted audience tactics.
