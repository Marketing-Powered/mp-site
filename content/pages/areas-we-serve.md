### Local search
Focus on accurate business information and useful service and location content. Google says complete, accurate Business Profile information makes a business more likely to appear in relevant local results. That is a reason to address the basics, not a promise of placement. [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en-en).
### Paid search
Define the service, geography, landing page and qualified-inquiry criteria before expanding spend. Set a budget against a business objective, then establish what evidence would justify continuing, changing or stopping the campaign.
### Websites and conversion
Give prospective customers enough information to choose: what you offer, whom it fits, where it is available and how to contact you. Prioritize a usable mobile contact path over adding pages without a clear purpose.
### Measurement
Plan beyond clicks and form submissions. Decide which first-party records can support measurement of qualified opportunities, appointments or admissions. Distinguish measured outcomes from estimates, and identify gaps rather than filling them with assumptions.
How an engagement starts

Prepare these points for the call:
* Your priority services and areas served.
* The business problem you want marketing to address.

Do not send passwords, patient information, call recordings or sensitive form submissions with an initial request. Start with public pages and a general description of the problem.
