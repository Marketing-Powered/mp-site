Plan beyond clicks and form fills. Identify which first-party CRM or intake records verify qualified opportunities, appointments, or admissions, and keep measured outcomes separated from estimates.

How does admission attribution compare to lead-only reporting?
Lead-only reporting stops at the click or form submission, whereas attribution tracked to admission, not just lead, links regional spend to verified downstream outcomes. Comparing both models helps leadership direct budget toward services and cities that produce real commercial value.
* Primary PPC metric; Lead-only reporting: Cost per click or form fill; Attribution tracked to admission: Conversion value per cost
* Downstream visibility; Lead-only reporting: Ends at inquiry submission; Attribution tracked to admission: Connects spend to CRM, appointment, or admission
* Multi-location clarity; Lead-only reporting: Blends sites into one average; Attribution tracked to admission: Separates demand quality by service and location
* Budget allocation; Lead-only reporting: Optimizes for raw inquiry volume; Attribution tracked to admission: Shifts spend toward verified revenue and capacity
