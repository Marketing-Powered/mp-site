### Not being found for the services you offer
Start with local search and technical SEO.
The assignment can include reviewing your Google Business Profile, service-page structure, internal navigation, business information and technical accessibility.
Google recommends complete, accurate business information and explains that profile relevance depends on how well the information matches a search. [Google Business Profile guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en-en)
For West Palm Beach local search, define the service and geography together. Separate a service delivered at a particular facility from one delivered throughout Palm Beach County. Do not ask a single broad page to explain every service and location.
Useful deliverables: a prioritized technical assessment, service-to-page map, profile corrections and a page-level implementation plan.
Choose this starting point when discovery is the question. If relevant visitors already reach the right page, investigate the inquiry path next.
### Traffic that does not turn into inquiries
Start with the landing page, paid search or both.
For Google Ads management, the scope should connect search intent, campaign structure, ad messaging and destination pages. For website work, focus on the information and actions a buyer needs.
Ask whether the page clearly explains:
* The service and whom it fits.
* Where it is available.
* What happens after a call or form submission.
* Which next step the visitor should choose.
Useful deliverables: campaign restructuring, service-page copy, landing-page development, form improvements and mobile inquiry-path testing.
We recommend separating a necessary rebuild from targeted repairs. A confusing service page does not automatically justify replacing the whole website.
### Not knowing which channel produced an inquiry
Start with measurement design.
Define the events before choosing tools: an inquiry, a qualified opportunity and the business outcome you need to assess.
The scope can cover call and form tracking, campaign-source capture, CRM or intake handoffs and reporting definitions. First-party data should support a decision, not simply populate another dashboard.
Useful deliverables: a measurement map, implementation requirements, event testing and reporting that distinguishes measured outcomes from estimates.
For healthcare and other sensitive work, establish permitted data, access and destinations before implementation. Keep patient records and confidential customer information out of an initial marketing inquiry.
A West Palm Beach inquiry journey worth examining
Observation date: November 16, 2026. This example comes from an AI-assisted browser review of the extracted public text on an area healthcare provider’s contact and appointment pages. No forms were submitted, and no private analytics or account data were accessed.
The contact page lists several West Palm Beach facilities alongside other locations, with separate telephone numbers and repeated appointment links. That provides location-specific contact information rather than only a general corporate destination. [Public contact-page example](https://paleyinstitute.org/contact-us/)
The appointment link leads to a form that asks which physician the visitor is requesting. Its introduction explains that the organization will respond to schedule an appointment, distinguishing a request from a confirmed booking. [Public appointment-page example](https://paleyinstitute.org/schedule-an-appointment/)
The decision gap to test: can someone who knows the service and preferred location, but not a physician’s name, confidently choose the next step?
Our recommendation would be to test a service-and-location selector before the detailed form, with an explicit option for visitors who need help choosing. Carry those selections into the request where technically appropriate.
This is a proposed improvement, not a finding about conversion performance or an agency result. Public page text cannot establish abandonment, routing accuracy or appointment volume.
For a Palm Beach County multi-location business, the practical lesson is specific: preserve the visitor’s service and location choices from the first page through the inquiry handoff.
