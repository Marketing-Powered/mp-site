Our Founder's Story
Marketing Powered exists because founder Mike Hulick spent years on the operator side of behavioral health, facing the same problems you face now. As CMO at Haven Health Management, Mike led marketing through a growth period that took the network from 3 locations to 24. Revenue scaled from roughly $30M to $140M during his tenure. That kind of expansion teaches you what works at scale and what breaks under pressure.
The experience also revealed a gap in the market. Most agencies selling to treatment centers had never managed a $1.5M to $2M monthly ad budget in the behavioral health space. They understood marketing theory but not the operational reality of admission calls, intake bandwidth, census management, and the compliance constraints that shape every campaign decision.
The agency operates with an operator's perspective: budgets are managed with the same discipline required when your own P&L is on the line. Attribution is tracked through to admission, not just to the lead form. Compliance is built into campaign architecture from day one, not bolted on after a policy violation.
Our Team
The Marketing Powered team brings specialized experience in behavioral health and mental health verticals. Every strategist on the team understands the nuances of sensitive healthcare advertising: the restrictions on retargeting, the LegitScript certification requirements, and the Google Ads policies that can suspend an account overnight if you don't know the rules.
The team has collectively managed over $50M in behavioral health and mental health media spend. That depth of experience means fewer mistakes, faster optimization cycles, and campaign structures that survive platform policy audits. We've seen what happens when agencies without vertical expertise run behavioral health campaigns. The learning curve is expensive, and the compliance risks are real.
Collaboration at Marketing Powered follows a simple principle: every client engagement gets senior-level attention, not a junior account manager learning on your budget. The team structure keeps accounts small enough that the people building your campaigns are the same people analyzing your data and recommending strategic shifts. No handoffs, no translation errors, no diluted expertise.
Why Choose Us
Marketing Powered operates differently from traditional agencies and from the growing number of AI tool vendors entering the space. The difference starts with AI-native operations built on proprietary infrastructure, not wrappers on commercial APIs. The agency has worked with local large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and vector databases since 2022, well before the current wave of AI marketing tools.
That technical foundation supports faster iteration, smarter optimization, and data sovereignty that matters in healthcare-adjacent verticals. Your data stays in a controlled infrastructure. Your campaigns benefit from AI-driven insights without the compliance exposure that comes from sending sensitive information through third-party systems.
The combination of operator experience, behavioral health marketing expertise, and AI-native infrastructure creates an agency that can execute at scale while respecting the constraints of your vertical. We understand why you can't retarget. We understand why your creative needs careful review. We understand why your intake team's capacity matters more than raw lead volume. That understanding shapes every campaign we build.
Our Commitment to Compliance
Behavioral health marketing operates under strict compliance requirements, and Marketing Powered treats those requirements as non-negotiable. The agency maintains LegitScript awareness and builds campaigns that respect Google's sensitive healthcare vertical policies from the start. No retargeting. No prohibited claims. No shortcuts that put your certification or your reputation at risk.
HIPAA awareness runs through every process. Marketing Powered's infrastructure is built with data sovereignty in mind, keeping protected health information in controlled environments rather than routing it through external systems. When we discuss attribution and tracking, we do so within the bounds of what is appropriate for healthcare marketing.
The paid media campaigns we deploy are structured for compliance first, performance second. That order matters. An account suspension doesn't just cost you leads for a week. It damages your quality history, your auction dynamics, and your relationship with the platform. We build campaigns that perform within the rules rather than chase short-term metrics that create long-term risk.
