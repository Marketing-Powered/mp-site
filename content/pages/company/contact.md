What happens on the call
You talk with an operator, not a sales rep. We walk through your current acquisition channels, how leads are tracked, and where investment stops connecting to results. You leave with a clear read on your measurement gaps, whether or not we work together.
Who this conversation is built for
Hospitals and health systems, healthcare organizations, multi-location businesses, and professional services firms in regulated markets. If your marketing has to survive compliance review and still produce qualified demand, this is the right room.
The questions we will ask you
Expect specifics. Where does a new patient or client inquiry enter your systems? Who answers the phone, and how fast? Can you trace a closed deal or an admission back to the campaign that produced it? The answers shape everything that follows.
Why operator experience changes the conversation
Marketing Powered is led by a former healthcare CMO who ran marketing for a treatment network that grew from 3 to 24 locations and managed $1.5 to $2M per month in Google Ads across 11+ behavioral and mental health accounts. The founder is also a court-certified expert witness in advertising strategy. That history shapes how we evaluate your accounts.
How we handle your data
We run owned, local AI infrastructure for HIPAA-aware inference, and we build for Google's sensitive healthcare vertical, where retargeting is not permitted. Attribution is tracked to admission, not just lead, when your data supports it.
