Who This Call Is For
This consultation fits operators with real acquisition stakes: a hospital or health system marketing lead staring at a media budget nobody can tie to admissions, a multi-location executive trying to make eleven markets behave like one system, or a professional services partner whose website produces inquiries but not engagements. If you own a number, this call is built for you. If you are shopping for the cheapest vendor, it probably is not.
What We Prepare Before You Arrive
A consultation is only useful if the diagnostic work starts before the meeting. Share your website and, where relevant, high-level context on your channels, and we review your visible funnel in advance: landing experience, tracking signals we can observe from the outside, local presence, and how your positioning compares with the competitors bidding against you. The call starts at the problem, not at introductions.
Experience That Has Managed the Budget You Are Describing
Marketing Powered is operator-led. The founder is a former healthcare CMO who ran marketing for a treatment network that grew from 3 to 24 locations, managed $1.5 to $2M per month in Google Ads across 11+ behavioral and mental health accounts, and serves as a court-certified expert witness in advertising strategy. That background shapes the consultation: the questions are the ones an operator asks, about cost per admitted or retained customer, not cost per form fill.
How the Consultation Runs, Step by Step
The call follows a working agenda. First, you walk through the business goal and the constraint, whether that is census, case volume, or expansion into new locations. Second, we examine the funnel together on screen: ad accounts if you choose to share them, landing pages, intake or call handling, and where attribution breaks. Third, we identify the highest-leverage gaps and talk honestly about sequence, trade-offs, and whether we are the right fit. No slideware, no scripted close.
Built for Regulated Acquisition From the Start
Healthcare and other regulated categories remove the shortcuts most agencies lean on. Google's health personalized advertising policy states that in sensitive health categories advertisers "can't use advertiser-curated audiences," which rules out conventional retargeting lists ([Google Advertising Policies Help](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701855?hl=en)). Marketing Powered is LegitScript-aware and HIPAA-aware, built for Google's sensitive healthcare vertical where retargeting is not permitted, so the strategies discussed on this call are ones you can actually run.
Measurement That Reaches the Outcome, Not the Lead
A lead count is an input, not a result. The measurement approach discussed on this call tracks attribution to admission, not just lead, connecting spend, channel, landing page, qualified opportunity, and the business outcome your board actually reads. Where data handling matters, Marketing Powered operates owned, local AI infrastructure for HIPAA-aware inference, so analysis does not require shipping sensitive context to third-party tools by default.
What You Leave With
You leave with three things in writing: the specific gaps we observed in your funnel, the order we would address them and why, and a candid read on fit, including when the right answer is not hiring us. Several operators have used that summary to brief their own teams. That is fine. Useful is the point.

FAQ
Q: How long is the consultation?
A: Plan for 45 to 60 minutes. Enough time to work the agenda above without padding.
Q: Who should join from our side?
A: Whoever owns the acquisition number, plus anyone who controls the website, CRM, or intake process. Decisions move faster when the operators are in the room.
Q: Is this a sales call?
