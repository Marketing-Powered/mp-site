FAQ
Q: What marketing strategies work for behavioral health providers?
A: Effective behavioral health marketing combines compliant paid media (Google Ads, Meta, within sensitive vertical restrictions), conversion-optimized landing pages, and attribution systems that track leads through to admission. The specific mix depends on your center's capacity, geography, and treatment modalities. Our AI-native marketing approach uses predictive analytics and multivariant optimization to allocate spend where it performs best.
Q: How does a marketing consultation benefit my center?
A: You get a candid assessment of your current marketing performance from operators who have managed $50M+ in behavioral health media spend. We identify gaps in attribution, compliance risks, and channel opportunities specific to your situation. The consultation is designed to give you actionable insight, whether or not you engage us for ongoing work.
Q: What should I prepare before the call?
A: Bring your current monthly ad spend, cost per lead or cost per admission if you track it, and a list of your primary growth goals. If you have access to your Google Ads or Meta accounts, having login credentials available can make the conversation more specific. Questions about compliance, lead quality, or attribution are always welcome.
Q: Is the consultation free?
A: Yes. The initial consultation is complimentary. We use this time to understand your business, assess fit, and determine whether our approach aligns with your goals. There is no obligation to proceed after the call.
