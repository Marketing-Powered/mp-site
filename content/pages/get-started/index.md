Tailored Marketing Strategies for Behavioral Health
Behavioral health marketing operates under constraints that most agencies do not understand. Google Ads classifies addiction treatment as a [sensitive healthcare vertical](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031), which means no retargeting. [LegitScript certification](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/) is required for addiction treatment advertising. HIPAA awareness must be built into every data flow.
We have operated under these restrictions for years. Our campaigns are built for compliance from the start, not patched after a policy violation takes down your account.
The results speak through marketing metrics: reduced cost per qualified call, higher admission rates from paid channels, and attribution models that connect media spend to census outcomes. Whether you run a single outpatient clinic or a multi-state residential network, the strategy adapts to your scale and your compliance posture.
