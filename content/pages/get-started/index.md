Get Started with Our Marketing Agency
Starting with a new marketing partner should feel like a diagnosis, not a pitch. Marketing Powered begins with your numbers, your intake process, and your constraints before anyone talks about channels. Here is exactly what happens when you reach out, and what you should have ready.

What the First Conversation Actually Looks Like
You talk to an operator, not a sales development rep reading a script. The first call covers three things: where your demand comes from today, what a new customer or admission is worth, and where tracking breaks between click and revenue. Bring whatever you have, even if it is messy. Ad account access, a CRM export, call logs, or a spreadsheet of monthly volume all work. The goal of the first hour is a shared picture of your acquisition economics, not a proposal.
Who Should Start a Conversation
Marketing Powered works with hospitals and health systems, behavioral health organizations, multi-location healthcare services, and professional services firms in regulated markets. The common thread is that the cost of a wrong marketing decision is high: expensive clicks, long sales or intake cycles, and compliance exposure if advertising is handled carelessly. If your growth depends on paid media, local visibility, and an intake or sales process that converts, the fit conversation is worth having.
Operator-Led Means the Operator Has Done the Job
The founder is a former healthcare CMO who ran marketing for a treatment network that grew from 3 to 24 locations, managing $1.5 to $2M per month in Google Ads across 11+ behavioral and mental health accounts. The founder is also a court-certified expert witness in advertising strategy. That history shapes how engagements start: with scrutiny of spend efficiency and measurement, because those are the levers an executive is accountable for. The firm has been AI-native since 2022, and AI is applied where it improves execution, not as the headline.
How We Scope Work: From Spend to Admission
Most agencies report leads. The difference between a lead report and a revenue report is the entire engagement. Scoping starts by mapping your funnel end to end: ad spend, landing page, form or call, qualification, and the admission or signed engagement at the end. Attribution is tracked to admission, not just lead, which changes what gets optimized. A campaign that produces cheap form fills and no admissions gets cut. One that produces fewer, costlier inquiries that convert gets more budget. You see that logic in the scoping document before work begins, so you can judge the logic before committing.
Built for Regulated Advertising From Day One
Healthcare advertising runs under real platform constraints. Google treats health as a sensitive interest category in personalized advertising, and advertiser-curated audiences such as remarketing lists and customer match cannot be used for it, which rules out conventional retargeting [Google Ads policy help](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701855?hl=en). For addiction treatment providers, LegitScript's addiction treatment certification is recognized by Google, Microsoft, and Meta as a gateway to advertising [LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/addiction-treatment-certification/). Marketing Powered is LegitScript-aware and HIPAA-aware, and builds acquisition plans that work inside these rules rather than around them.
Your Data Stays Yours, Processed on Owned Infrastructure
When AI touches your marketing data, where that inference runs matters. Marketing Powered operates owned, local AI infrastructure for HIPAA-aware inference, so sensitive workflows can run on hardware the firm controls rather than defaulting everything to third-party tools. First-party data work follows the same principle: your CRM records, call tracking, and analytics remain your assets, structured so you keep them if the engagement ever ends. Data sovereignty is discussed in scoping, not discovered later.
