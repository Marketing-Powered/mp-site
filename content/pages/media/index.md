Navigating Compliance in Media Relations
Media coverage in behavioral health requires the same compliance discipline as advertising. Every public statement, interview, and press mention must respect the boundaries that govern healthcare marketing: no outcome guarantees, no patient-identifiable information, no claims that could be construed as medical advice.
Marketing Powered applies LegitScript and HIPAA awareness to media relations with the same rigor applied to paid media campaigns. When participating in interviews or contributing expert commentary, we frame outcomes as marketing performance metrics, such as cost per admission lead, qualified call volume, and attribution accuracy,  rather than treatment efficacy claims.
This compliance posture protects both Marketing Powered and the treatment centers we serve. Media coverage that crosses regulatory lines creates liability. Coverage that respects boundaries builds durable credibility.
