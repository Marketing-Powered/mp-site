Court-certified expert witness work in advertising strategy
Marketing Powered's founder is a court-certified expert witness in advertising strategy. That status comes from a court, not from a website bio. A court examines knowledge, skill, experience, training, or education, and whether an opinion rests on a method someone else can test. Opposing counsel is paid to find the weak joint: an unsupported number, a strategy written after the fact, or a result that was only an estimate.
Campaign logic, keyword choices, and budget decisions get set out for a reader who does not work in marketing. If a claim cannot survive that reading, it should not have shipped. The habit carries into the work Marketing Powered does now. Document why a strategy was chosen. Keep measured results apart from estimates. Decline a figure that cannot be defended line by line.

Operating inside LegitScript and Google's sensitive healthcare rules
Google restricts the promotion of recovery-oriented drug and alcohol addiction services ([Addiction services policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15598649)). [[1]](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15598649) In the United States, an advertiser must be certified as an addiction services provider by the LegitScript Certification program and must also be certified with Google before those ads can run. Providers that are not eligible for LegitScript certification are not allowed to advertise drug and alcohol addiction services on Google ([Healthcare and medicines policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031)). [[2]](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031)
Google lists health as a sensitive interest category, including counseling for depression, anxiety, and addiction. Advertiser-curated audiences are not allowed. That includes Customer Match, your data segments, audience expansion, and lookalike segments. Predefined Google audiences can still be used ([Health in personalized advertising](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701855)). [[3]](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701855) Marketing Powered is LegitScript-aware and HIPAA-aware, and built for Google's sensitive healthcare vertical, where retargeting is not permitted.
Google will issue a warning at least 7 days before suspending an account for a violation of the addiction services policy, rather than suspending immediately without warning ([Addiction services policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15598649)). [[1]](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15598649) That notice is not spare time to invent a compliance story. A live disapproval still removes the demand a census plan was counting on.
