Pitch guidelines for media and press
These interview guidelines explain how journalists, editors, and podcast producers can submit a media pitch to Marketing Powered for interviews, commentary, and editorial collaboration. The page outlines the four topics we cover with direct operating depth, the exact details every press inquiry needs, and the requests we decline. Reviewing these standards first saves time on both sides and moves qualified stories forward cleanly.

What we cover
Marketing Powered responds to media inquiries where direct operational experience adds concrete detail that generic marketing commentary misses. Coverage centers on four areas:
* Accountable paid media. Evaluating [paid media](/services/paid-media/) programs against qualified demand, conversion value per cost, and completed admissions rather than ad-platform lead totals, with [attribution tracked to admission](/services/analytics-and-attribution/), not just lead.
* Regulated-industry advertising. Operating acquisition programs inside Google's sensitive healthcare vertical, where personalized advertising and remarketing lists are restricted for sensitive health categories ([Google Advertising Policies Help](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701855)), alongside LegitScript-aware and HIPAA-aware marketing workflows.
* Multi-location and healthcare growth. Running local demand capture, multi variant optimization on landing pages, and intake economics for [hospitals, health systems, and multi-site healthcare organizations](/who-we-help/hospitals-and-health-systems/), as well as professional services and other regulated markets.
* Practical AI in marketing operations. Applying AI-native workflows built since 2022, including owned, local AI infrastructure for HIPAA-aware inference, examined through data sovereignty, governance, and operating cost rather than software hype.
Stories examining how ad spend, conversion systems, and downstream revenue connect across these four areas are our primary focus.
Who you will be pitching
Interview and commentary requests go directly to [Marketing Powered's founder](/company/about/), speaking from firsthand operating experience rather than agency PR talking points.
Stated strictly as the founder's own prior career history rather than agency results, that background includes serving as a former healthcare CMO who ran marketing for a treatment network that grew from 3 to 24 locations and managing Google Ads across 11+ behavioral and mental health accounts. The founder is also a court-certified expert witness in advertising strategy and has operated AI-native workflows since 2022.
For editors and producers, that distinction shapes the interview: commentary reflects direct accountability for multi-location growth targets, regulated paid search rules, and formal legal scrutiny of advertising strategy.
What makes a pitch worth a response
Media pitches that earn a reply share three traits: specific outlet context, alignment with our four coverage areas, and a defined editorial ask. Mass template outreach sent unchanged to dozens of marketing firms is skipped.
* Specificity over volume. Name the publication or show, define the audience, and state the exact question your story answers. Generic requests for broad marketing predictions rarely move forward.
* Alignment with regulated operations. Stories covering healthcare ad policy, click-to-admission attribution gaps, multi-location acquisition economics, or data control in AI workflows receive careful review. Social-media fads and generic branding roundups do not.
* Defined editorial format. A recorded podcast episode, background policy briefing, written Q&A, or interview examining a specific commercial trade-off gives us clear terms to evaluate. Open-ended requests to "hop on a quick intro call" do not.
Skeptical reporting is welcome. If your piece examines misaligned agency incentives, overstated AI claims, or measurement gaps in regulated advertising, the founder will engage with the trade-offs directly.
What to include in your pitch
Submit your media pitch through the inquiry form linked at the bottom of this page, and start your message with "Media Pitch" so it routes straight to editorial review. Keep the note under 250 words and include five items:
* Outlet and audience. Name the publication, newsletter, broadcast, or podcast, and describe who reads, watches, or listens.
* Story angle. Summarize your thesis or episode premise in two or three clear sentences rather than a loose list of keywords.
* Why this founder fits. Connect your angle to the founder's operating history in multi-location healthcare scaling, regulated paid search, admission-level attribution, or advertising strategy expert-witness work.
* Format and commitment. Specify whether you need a written Q&A, recorded interview, live panel, or background call, plus the expected time window.
* Deadlines. Provide both the cutoff date to confirm participation and the final deadline for recording or copy delivery.
Links to your outlet and one or two recent pieces tell us far more than attached PDFs or generic press decks.
Topics we are not the right fit for
Clear boundaries protect editorial deadlines on both sides. Marketing Powered declines backlink swaps, pay-to-play placements, unrelated consumer verticals, and clinical commentary so reporters can move immediately to the right source.
* Accountable paid media, attribution, or regulated ad policy; Fit status: In scope; Reason: Matches direct operating and expert-witness history
* Multi-location healthcare or regulated-market acquisition; Fit status: In scope; Reason: Aligns with multi-site marketing and intake economics
* Backlink swaps and contributed SEO guest posts; Fit status: Declined; Reason: Not journalism or editorial commentary; no replies sent
* Pay-to-play spotlights and fee-based industry awards; Fit status: Declined; Reason: Outside editorial press standards
* Unrelated consumer, lifestyle, or crypto roundups; Fit status: Declined; Reason: No tie to regulated acquisition, attribution, or AI governance
* Clinical diagnosis, medical protocols, or treatment efficacy; Fit status: Declined; Reason: Belongs with licensed clinicians, not marketing operators
If an angle sits near the boundary, send a two-sentence summary first. A fast, direct decline lets you move to a better-matched source without losing a reporting cycle.
How we review and respond
Every inquiry labeled "Media Pitch" goes through a three-step human review covering intake verification, scope and schedule matching, and a direct reply. We prioritize submissions with explicit deadlines and do not publish a fixed response window.
* Intake and routing. The submission is logged and checked for the five required details, with press deadlines flagged immediately.
* Scope and calendar check. The pitch is evaluated against our four coverage areas and matched against upcoming operating schedule availability.
* Direct reply. You receive a clear yes, a brief clarifying question, or a polite decline so neither side wastes cycles.
Once accepted, we send a scheduling link and verified background notes on the founder's history to support accurate reporting. One short follow-up after a few days is fine if you have not heard back, though daily pings do not speed up review.

FAQ
Q: Do you accept guest post or backlink exchange requests?
A: No. These guidelines cover media interviews, podcasts, expert commentary, and editorial collaboration with journalists and producers. Link-exchange outreach and contributed SEO guest posts are declined without a reply.
Q: Can I pitch a story outside healthcare?
A: Yes, provided the angle connects to accountable paid media, multi-location acquisition, marketing attribution, or regulated-industry advertising. While the founder's deepest career history is in healthcare, those mechanics also apply to professional services and other regulated sectors.
Q: Who will I actually be interviewing?
A: You will interview [Marketing Powered's founder](/company/about/), a former healthcare CMO and court-certified expert witness in advertising strategy. The founder's personal career history includes scaling a treatment network from 3 to 24 locations and managing Google Ads across 11+ behavioral and mental health accounts.
Q: How fast will I get a response?
A: There is no fixed turnaround commitment because active operating schedules vary week to week. Submissions with clear deadlines are reviewed ahead of open-ended inquiries, and urgent timelines flagged at the start of the message are triaged first.
Q: What if my publication or podcast serves a niche audience?
A: Audience relevance matters more than total reach. A focused trade publication or podcast read by hospital executives, multi-location operators, or regulated-market decision makers is often a stronger fit than a broad general-interest outlet.
