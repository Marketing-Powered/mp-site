In-House or Agency Marketing for a Peptide Business
Most peptide marketing agency vs in house comparisons open with a salary on one side and a retainer on the other, which is the least useful number in the decision. What actually moves the outcome is review capacity, who holds the accounts and the data, and what a single eligibility error costs. This page gives you a way to score that with your own inputs.

Define the five jobs hiding inside one marketing line item
"Marketing" is not one function in this category. It is five, and they need different people.
Eligibility and product-status review. Someone has to determine, in writing, what the business actually does and what each channel and processor will accept from that activity. LegitScript assesses applicants on their activities rather than on category labels, and the Healthcare Merchant Certification program is aimed at businesses involved in the online sale, prescribing, facilitation or payment processing of prescription medications and certain over-the-counter medications ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)). It also lists research-use-only chemical businesses, including peptides, among those generally not eligible, while noting that some businesses in those categories may still qualify if they also perform other in-scope activities ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)). That single distinction changes the entire acquisition plan.
Creative production. Offers, landing pages, video, email and lifecycle assets, produced at a cadence that keeps cost per acquisition from drifting.
Acquisition execution. Campaign structure, bidding, audience and feed management, measurement hygiene.
Data and attribution ownership. First-party capture, CRM mapping, consent handling, and the connection between spend and revenue rather than spend and form fills.
Incident response. Disapprovals, verification requests, processor reviews and appeals. Google treats attempts to circumvent its ad review processes as egregious violations, with suspension on detection and without prior warning ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075?hl=en)). The work of responding correctly is specialist work, and it rarely sits in a job description.
Lump these five into one headcount or one retainer and you cannot tell which capability you are actually buying.
Assess internal capability before comparing salaries and retainers
Write the five jobs down the left side of a page. Beside each one, name a person, not a department. If a name repeats three times, you have a concentration risk. If a box is empty, you have a gap that neither a hire nor a vendor will fix unless you scope it explicitly.
Then test the names with evidence questions rather than confidence questions:
* Has this person read the current policy text for the channels you run, or a summary of it written by someone else?
* Has this person ever assembled a verification submission with supporting business documents?
* Has this person handled an appeal, and what happened?
* Who decides whether a specific product page can be promoted, and where is that decision recorded?
* Who would hold the line if a growth target depended on advertising something the channel does not permit?
Most teams discover the same pattern. Creative and execution capability is present or hireable. Eligibility judgment and incident response are not, because few marketers have done that work repeatedly in a restricted category. That is a peptide marketing capability assessment, and it is the input that should drive the staffing decision.
For the baseline structural trade-offs that apply to any category, read the general comparison of [in-house vs. agency marketing](/resources/in-house-vs-agency-marketing). This page is the layer on top of it: in a restricted category, the binding constraint is review capacity, not speed or price.
Keep four statuses separate while you score yourself. Operating lawfully, product regulatory status, certification status and channel account status are four different things, and strength in one does not create eligibility in another.
Model the full operating cost with your own inputs
Build the model before you take a proposal. Use your numbers, not published averages, and label every assumption you cannot source.
* Fully loaded internal salaries. Input you supply: Base plus taxes, benefits, equipment, recruiting cost, amortized.
* Management time. Input you supply: Hours per week from you or a senior leader, priced at your rate.
* Vendor fees. Input you supply: Retainer, percentage of spend, project fees, minimum terms.
* Certification. Input you supply: Application and annual fees, counted per root domain.
* Tooling and infrastructure. Input you supply: Analytics, call tracking, CRM, hosting, consent management.
* Legal and regulatory review. Input you supply: Outside counsel hours for product and claim language.
* Incident response. Input you supply: Estimated events per year, response hours, revenue at risk during downtime.
* Ramp and vacancy. Input you supply: Weeks to productivity, expected turnover, coverage during gaps.
Two inputs are commonly understated. The first is certification, which LegitScript prices per root domain at a $975 application fee and a $2,150 annual certification fee due only on approval ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)). Multi-brand operators should multiply that by the number of root domains they intend to run, since certification applies at the root domain level ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)).
The second is incident response. Treat it as a recurring line with a frequency estimate you are willing to defend, not as an exception. Any frequency you assume here is an estimate, so write it in the model as one and revisit it with your actual event log after two quarters.
For channel-level cost modeling detail, including how management models change effective cost at different spend levels, see the guide to [PPC management models](/resources/ppc-management). Peptide marketing team costs only become comparable once both columns contain the same eight rows.
Compare control, access and continuity
Run the comparison as a handover test. Picture the person who owns your ad accounts leaving on a Friday, whether that person is an employee or a vendor contact, and list what you still hold on Monday morning.
You should hold the advertising accounts under an entity you control, with your vendor granted access rather than owning the container. You should hold the domains, the tag and consent configuration, the CRM, the call tracking numbers, the creative files in editable format, and the raw event data behind every reported number. If a dashboard is the only place a metric exists, you do not own the measurement.
Documentation is the part teams skip. Keep a dated record of every eligibility decision, the evidence submitted during verification, the business documents used, the policy text relied on, and who approved the call. This matters more than it looks: Google's best practices for multiple account abuse include resolving existing suspensions through appeal and contacting the agency or third party that manages your account, which means account history and relationships follow a business across vendors ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075?hl=en)). Submitting false information in verification programs is prohibited, and advertisers who cannot be verified after three attempts lose the ability to appeal a suspension ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075?hl=en)). Accurate records are an operational asset, not paperwork.
Certification continuity has its own owner requirement. LegitScript asks certified businesses to report significant changes, including ownership, business operations, partnerships, major website updates, new services, expansion into new jurisdictions, and licensing or regulatory actions ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)). Someone internal has to notice those events. Use the [compliance documentation standards](/resources/healthcare-marketing-compliance-checklist) as the control reference when you assign that.
Design a hybrid model that keeps judgment in house
The strongest structure for most peptide operators is not binary. Keep judgment and memory inside the business. Buy capacity and specialist review outside it.
Keep in house: the final approval on what may be promoted, the relationship with counsel, the certification record and change reporting, ownership of accounts and data, and the register of decisions. These are the things that compound and the things that are expensive to reconstruct.
Outsource: production volume, channel execution, analytics engineering, and periodic independent review of eligibility posture by someone who reads primary policy and certification sources rather than summaries.
Make the boundary explicit in a responsibility matrix with four columns per job: who does the work, who approves, who is informed, and where the output is stored. Ambiguity in that matrix is where most failures originate, because both sides assume the other is watching the policy.
Two conditions make a hybrid peptide agency operating model work. First, the internal approver must have authority to stop a campaign, including one with revenue attached. Second, the external team must document its reasoning rather than deliver conclusions, so that the decision survives a change of vendor.
Where certification is part of your path, understand the pathway before you divide responsibilities, since it determines what evidence you must produce and maintain. The [LegitScript certification process](/resources/legitscript-certification) guide covers that detail. Note that LegitScript also directs businesses whose sites include substance use disorder treatment information toward Addiction Treatment Certification rather than Healthcare Merchant Certification, so scoping the right program is itself a decision with consequences ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)).
Set acceptance criteria before you hire or sign
Decide what proof looks like while you still have leverage. Apply the same standard to a candidate and to a vendor.
Service boundaries in writing. Which of the five jobs is covered, which is excluded, and what response time applies to a disapproval, a verification request or a processor review.
Named accountability. The individual who performs the work, not the team that was presented during the pitch.
Demonstrated review capability. Ask for an eligibility assessment of your actual catalogue before signing, including the sources relied on. A vendor who answers with category generalities rather than your specific activities is not doing the work LegitScript itself does when it reviews a business.
Exit terms. Notice period, data export format, account access transfer, documentation handover and creative file delivery, all specified before the first invoice.

Questions buyers ask about peptide marketing staffing
### Does a sensitive vertical always need an agency?
No. The requirement is capability and review capacity, not vendor status. A business with an experienced internal lead who reads primary policy sources, maintains the certification record and handles appeals can run this well in house. A business without that person is exposed whether or not it signs a vendor, because the gap is in review, not in execution hours.
### Which responsibilities should remain with the business?
Final approval on what may be promoted, the certification record and the obligation to report significant business, website and licensing changes ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)), ownership of accounts, domains and first-party data, the relationship with counsel, and the written register of eligibility decisions. Everything else can be bought.
### How should total cost be calculated?
Build one model with the same line items for both options: fully loaded salaries, management time, vendor fees, certification fees per root domain, tooling, legal review, incident response and ramp or vacancy cost. Use your own figures and mark estimates as estimates. Certification carries published fees of $975 to apply and $2,150 annually per root domain on approval ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/healthcare-certification/faq/)); most other inputs are specific to your structure.
### Can a hybrid team work?
Yes, when the split follows judgment rather than seniority. A common structure is an internal owner holding approvals, certification reporting and data, with external capacity covering production, channel execution and independent eligibility review. It fails when the internal approver lacks authority to stop a campaign or when the external team delivers conclusions without documented reasoning.
### What evidence should justify outsourcing to a peptide marketing partner?
A written eligibility assessment of your actual activities before contracting, named individuals accountable for each job, documented response commitments for disapprovals and verification requests, exit terms covering data and account transfer, and a refusal to promise reinstatement or blanket eligibility. Google states that accounts are reinstated only in compelling circumstances ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075?hl=en)), so any vendor pitching assured recovery is describing something outside their control.
