What makes healthcare PPC measurement different?
Healthcare PPC measurement should connect paid inquiries with authorized intake outcomes while accounting for service fit, location capacity and restrictions on data use. The distinction is not that other industries cannot measure offline outcomes. Google Ads supports offline conversion imports. [Google Ads offline conversion documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2998031?hl=en).
For this engagement, the reporting standard is:
* What happened?; Lead-only reporting: A call or form submission; Outcome-based healthcare reporting: An inquiry reached a defined intake stage
* Was it relevant?; Lead-only reporting: Not established; Outcome-based healthcare reporting: Qualification assessed against agreed criteria
* Where did it happen?; Lead-only reporting: Campaign total; Outcome-based healthcare reporting: Location and service line
* What informs the next decision?; Lead-only reporting: Inquiry volume and cost; Outcome-based healthcare reporting: Qualification, confirmed outcomes and capacity
* What remains unknown?; Lead-only reporting: Often omitted; Outcome-based healthcare reporting: Unmatched records and pending outcomes identified
A useful report makes uncertainty visible. An inquiry without a confirmed intake status should remain unresolved, not be counted as a successful acquisition.
From qualified inquiry to admission or appointment
Start with definitions your marketing and intake teams can maintain:
* Inquiry: A distinct inbound contact.
* Qualified inquiry: Meets agreed service, geographic and other relevant eligibility criteria.
* Accepted or scheduled: Reaches the organization’s defined acceptance or booking stage.
* Admitted or attended: Confirmed in the appropriate operational record.
Calculate cost per qualified inquiry as paid search spend divided by qualified inquiries. Calculate media cost per admitted patient using confirmed admissions attributed to that spend. Keep scheduled and attended appointments separate.
Do not label a media-only calculation as fully loaded patient acquisition cost. Define which agency, technology and other acquisition expenses are included.
### A worked measurement example
Illustrative scenario, not client data or a performance benchmark.
Suppose locations have equal spending and call volume, but different qualification and admission totals. Their cost per call is equal; their downstream acquisition costs are not.
Investigate the reason before reallocating budget:
* Wrong-service inquiries; Account or operational response to evaluate: Review search terms, negatives and landing-page language
* Relevant inquiries left unanswered; Account or operational response to evaluate: Review routing and staffing coverage
* Qualified inquiries without capacity; Account or operational response to evaluate: Adjust promotion around actual availability
* Missing intake outcomes; Account or operational response to evaluate: Repair the reporting handoff before drawing conclusions
Intake owns outcome accuracy. Marketing owns translating permitted feedback into campaign decisions. Both need agreed definitions and an owner for unresolved records.

Location-level reporting that supports budget decisions
Compare locations using consistent definitions and reporting periods. Include spend, qualified inquiries, confirmed outcomes and unresolved records alongside service availability and inquiry-handling context.
Keep attribution windows explicit. An inquiry generated this period may reach admission later. Separate incomplete reporting periods from mature ones, and distinguish measured outcomes from estimates.
The operating question is not simply which location has the lowest reported cost. It is where additional investment is supportable, given demand quality, capacity and confidence in the data.
Policy review and data boundaries
For sensitive-interest categories, Google prohibits advertiser-curated audiences, including Customer Match and advertisers’ own data segments. Some predefined Google audiences remain available. [Google personalized advertising policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465?hl=en).
For recovery-oriented drug and alcohol addiction services advertising targeting the United States, Google requires LegitScript certification and Google certification. [Google healthcare and medicines policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031?hl=en).
Conversion-value design begins by separating internal measurement from platform-eligible signals. Google prohibits sensitive health conversions in enhanced conversions and store sales uploads. Consent alone does not create an exception. [Google customer data policies](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7475709?hl=en).
We do not assume admissions or appointment outcomes can be uploaded. Before implementing tracking, the work considers applicable privacy requirements, permitted disclosures, vendor arrangements and platform restrictions. HIPAA obligations apply when covered entities or business associates collect or disclose PHI through tracking technologies. [HHS tracking technology guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/hipaa-online-tracking/index.html).

FAQ
Q: How should healthcare PPC be measured against qualified calls or admissions?
A: Use agreed qualification criteria, then calculate acquisition costs against confirmed outcomes by location. Show missing records and reporting lag rather than treating incomplete attribution as complete.
Q: Can you work with an existing CRM or intake system?
A: Compatibility depends on system access, permitted data flows and engagement scope. Establish those boundaries before selecting an integration approach.
Q: Must intake outcomes be sent to Google?
A: No. Internal attribution and advertising-platform measurement are separate decisions. Platform eligibility must be assessed independently.
