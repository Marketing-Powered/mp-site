Integrating Facebook and Instagram Ads
Facebook and Instagram require different creative approaches, but they share a single auction system. Treating them as separate channels wastes budget and fragments your data. Our Facebook ads agency methodology unifies both platforms under a cohesive strategy while respecting the behavioral differences of each audience.
Instagram skews younger and favors visual storytelling. For mental health services, particularly [trauma and PTSD treatment](/who-we-help/mental-health/trauma-ptsd), this platform often outperforms Facebook for family member outreach and early-stage awareness. Facebook delivers stronger results for longer-form content, testimonial-style creative, and retargeting-adjacent sequences (within Meta's health advertising restrictions).


We structure campaigns to let Meta's algorithm allocate budget across placements dynamically, then analyze performance by placement to inform creative development. This approach captures efficiency gains from unified bidding while building platform-specific learnings over time. 


According to [Meta's own research](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/insights/the-value-of-optimizing-ad-delivery-across-facebook-and-instagram), advertisers who optimize across both platforms see stronger overall results than those who silo their campaigns.
