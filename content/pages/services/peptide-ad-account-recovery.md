Diagnose the platform restriction before editing anything
Peptide ad account recovery begins by reading the exact enforcement notice delivered inside the ad platform and by email. Suspensions, asset disapprovals, verification holds, and billing locks carry different evidence rules, so editing campaigns before classifying the restriction can erase audit trails or trigger fresh flags.
During intake, our paid media operators inspect the raw policy notice, billing ledger, verification logs, and active permissions before touching campaign settings. Suspended accounts typically lock into a restricted state limited to appeals, verification, billing, security, and reporting, blocking routine campaign changes immediately.
* Account suspension; Common trigger: Policy breach or linked-account violation; Operational effect: Delivery halts and campaign edits lock; Primary recovery path: Root-cause audit and single documented appeal
* Ad or keyword disapproval; Common trigger: Restricted terms or landing page mismatch; Operational effect: Flagged ads pause while eligible ads run; Primary recovery path: Copy edit or targeted dispute
* Verification hold; Common trigger: Unconfirmed entity or mismatched records; Operational effect: Delivery pauses pending identity clearance; Primary recovery path: Consistent corporate and signer documentation
* Billing hold; Common trigger: Unpaid balance or payment dispute; Operational effect: Spend stops until payment clears; Primary recovery path: Financial reconciliation and payment verification
Audit offers, destination pages, and linked accounts
Platform reviewers inspect the full commercial footprint behind an ad account rather than isolated headlines. Our review covers the peptide catalog, product descriptions, telehealth intake forms, checkout screens, corporate domains, and connected business manager or payment profiles.
Substance eligibility sits upstream of any ad copy change. Major ad networks enforce strict rules covering unapproved pharmaceuticals and restricted prescription compounds. When a landing page promotes a prohibited substance or unsupported medical claims, rewriting ad text alone will fail review.
Related-account exposure demands equal attention. Platforms connect accounts across shared payment profiles, legal registrations, verified identities, and domains, so one clinic suspension can cascade across affiliated profiles. Marketing Powered conducts these reviews as a LegitScript-aware and HIPAA-aware team built for Google's sensitive healthcare vertical, where retargeting is not permitted.
Pick one defensible path for every flagged issue
Every audit finding maps to one of four legitimate actions: fix and resubmit, dispute with verifiable records, exclude ineligible offers, or leave the channel for restricted products. Treating a prohibited offer like a minor copy error wastes appeal standing and raises permanent enforcement risk.
* Correct and resubmit: Revise noncompliant landing page copy or flagged creative assets when the underlying clinical service meets platform rules.
* Dispute with records: Challenge automated misclassification when an eligible clinic was flagged in error, backed by verifiable business and licensing records.
* Isolate restricted offers: Remove ineligible peptide listings from paid landing paths so compliant consultations or adjacent medical services can advertise cleanly.
* Reallocate budget: Stop advertising restricted products on channels that prohibit them and shift spend to permitted acquisition channels and organic search.
Launching replacement accounts or rotating domains is never a legitimate fifth path.
Assemble an evidence-backed appeal packet reviewers can verify
Platform appeal teams weigh verifiable evidence rather than persuasive copywriting. Our operators work alongside your marketing lead and compliance counsel to compile a factual submission where every stated fix ties directly to a dated site update or official corporate record.
* Archive the baseline: Save the exact suspension notice, cited policy text, account ID, and live screenshots of flagged destinations before making changes.
* Log dated remediations: Record removed SKUs, edited claims, updated checkout flows, and revised disclosures with timestamps so reviewers can verify the live destination.
* Align corporate and licensing files: Check legal registrations, domain records, payment profiles, and pharmacy or telehealth certifications such as LegitScript records so entity details match everywhere.
* Clear advertiser verification first: Confirm whether identity verification is required before appealing, because repeated verification failures can close the review window.
* Submit one concise appeal: Present verified corrections and supporting records tied directly to the cited policy without filler or speculation.
Coordinate reviewer communication without triggering repeat rejections
Filing duplicate appeals for the same suspension weakens recovery odds rather than accelerating review. Automated enforcement queues flag repetitive tickets as potential misuse, which can pause processing or bury valid updates while a single complete packet moves cleanly through review.
We manage platform communication in one shared tracker logging submission dates, case numbers, verification tasks, and reviewer questions. When a reviewer requests clarification, we supply exact, redacted records answering that specific point instead of refiling the entire history. Controlled pacing gives operations teams room to fix root causes before submitting.
Set clear recovery boundaries and ongoing governance
Reinstatement restores access to an advertising account at a single point in time. It does not approve future peptide SKUs or updated clinical claims, and the policies that triggered enforcement govern every campaign published afterward.
Ad platforms treat circumvention as a severe violation causing immediate suspension without warning. Prohibited tactics include creating new accounts after a suspension decision, rotating domains to evade review, masking destination pages, or submitting false verification records. Marketing Powered will not build workaround accounts to bypass review or alter compliance documentation to fit an appeal narrative.
