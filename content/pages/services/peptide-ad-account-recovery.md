Peptide Ad Account Recovery and Appeal Support
Peptide ad account recovery starts with a diagnosis, not an appeal form. A suspension, a disapproval, a verification hold and a billing hold are different platform states with different evidence requirements, and treating them the same way wastes weeks. We tell you which one you are in, what can honestly be fixed, and whether the business model supports advertising at all.

Identify the exact restriction before touching anything
The first working session is a read of the account notice, the email notification and the policy named in it. Google sends both an in-account notification and an email that identifies every policy cited in the suspension, plus a link to appeal, so that text is the primary evidence, not a summary of it. Suspended accounts go read-only with a narrow set of permitted actions: appeals, advertiser verification, billing, security settings and reporting access, which is why bulk edits and new campaign builds are off the table immediately ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). We separate four states: an account suspension, an ad or keyword disapproval, a verification or identity hold, and a billing or payment hold triggered by issues such as a chargeback, unpaid balance or suspicious payment activity ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). Each one routes to a different response.
Review product eligibility, destinations and account history
The account is rarely the whole problem. The review covers what is actually sold, how the destination page describes it, what claims appear in the checkout and consultation flow, and what the related accounts and payment identities look like. This matters because Google maintains a separate [unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/2423645?hl=en) policy covering substances it does not permit advertisers to promote, which sits upstream of any account question. We also map related accounts, since accounts related to a suspended account may themselves be suspended, and an account suspended because other accounts verified with the same identity documents were suspended can be reinstated automatically once those appeals succeed ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). The same discipline we apply to paid search structure in regulated accounts applies here, and our [Google Ads agency](/services/google-ads-agency) page describes the broader account review standard.
Choose the legitimate action for each finding
Four outcomes exist, and only four. Correct and resubmit when the violation was a fixable asset or landing page issue. Dispute with documentation when the enforcement appears to be an error. Exclude the offer and keep advertising the eligible part of the catalog when one product is the problem. Stop promoting the product through that channel when it is not eligible, and move budget to channels that permit it. There is no fifth option involving new accounts or alternate domains. For broader context on how Google and Meta treat regulated medical and wellness categories, read the [healthcare advertising policy guide](/resources/healthcare-advertising) before deciding where budget goes next.
Prepare a factual appeal with documents you can defend
Appeal preparation looks like evidence assembly, not persuasion writing. We work from a redacted intake checklist: the suspension notice, policy cited, a dated change log of what was altered on the site and in the account, business registration and ownership documents, any applicable certification records, and screenshots of the live destination as reviewers would see it. Google states that accounts are reinstated only in compelling circumstances, such as a mistake, which is why an accurate and complete submission matters more than tone ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). Certain advertisers must complete advertiser verification successfully in order to appeal, and if identity cannot be verified after three attempts, the appeal route closes ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). Documentation hygiene for regulated advertisers is covered in our [healthcare marketing compliance checklist](/resources/healthcare-marketing-compliance-checklist).
Manage official communication without burning the appeal route
Volume is not leverage. Filing repeated appeals for the same suspension can mean they are not processed, and indications of appeals misuse can pause processing of certain appeals for seven days ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). Advertisers have at least six months from the suspension date to submit an appeal, so a single accurate submission beats five rushed ones ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). We track submission dates, requests for additional information, verification tasks and outcomes in one place, and we respond to what the reviewer actually asked for. If certification is part of the eligibility question for your category, our [LegitScript certification](/resources/legitscript-certification) resource explains the cost, rules and timeline considerations.
Define the limits of recovery before any work begins
Reinstatement restores an account. It does not approve a product, a claim set or a future campaign, and the same policy that produced the suspension still governs what runs afterward. Circumvention is treated as an egregious violation: Google prohibits bypassing enforcement by creating variations of disapproved ads, domains or content, creating new accounts to re-enter the system after a suspension decision, and submitting false information as part of verification programs ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075?hl=en)). Egregious violations cause immediate suspension without prior warning ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9841640?hl=en)). We will not build replacement accounts, swap domains to evade review or shape verification documents to fit a story.
