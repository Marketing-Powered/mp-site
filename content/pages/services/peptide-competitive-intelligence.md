Private Competitive Intelligence for Peptide Businesses
In peptides, visible market activity and durable, lawful market position are not the same thing. Our peptide competitive intelligence work joins permitted public research with your own authorized business data, then states plainly what was observed, what was inferred and what is still unknown. Start from the commercial decision, not a competitor scan
Useful intelligence begins in a scoping conversation with the people who own the number: a founder weighing a second clinic line, a CMO deciding whether to fund a new offer, a commercial lead choosing between two acquisition targets. Before research starts, we write down the decision in one sentence, the date it has to be made, the budget attached to it and the evidence that would change the answer. A generic competitor sweep produces slides. A decision brief produces a shortlist of options and the specific facts that separate them. If the decision cannot be stated clearly, the research scope cannot be either, and that is worth resolving before anyone spends money on peptide competitor research.
Classify the competitor universe before you compare anyone
Peptide clinics, 503A compounding pharmacies, research-supply labs and consumer wellness brands are frequently lumped into one "competitive set" and then compared on price, ad volume or site polish. They do not operate under the same rules. The HHS enforcement discretion policy announced in a Federal Register notice on October 28, 2026 applies to qualifying 503A pharmacies and physician compounders, and 503B outsourcing facilities are not covered by it ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/healthcare/hhs-peptide-compounding-policy-what-healthcare-businesses-need-to-know/)). A business-model taxonomy comes first in any private peptide industry analysis, because comparing a telehealth prescriber to a research-only supplier on conversion rate is a false equivalence that quietly corrupts every number downstream. Public research here means public: published pages, visible pricing and offer language, publicly viewable ad transparency listings, published location counts, job postings and press announcements. Separate what was observed from what was inferred
This is the discipline that protects your budget. A live competitor ad is evidence that an ad passed review at a point in time. It is not evidence of revenue, margin, approval status or regulatory standing. LegitScript updated its certification review approach effective November 2, 2026 to reflect the policy's conditional allowances, while noting that the policy does not mean those peptides are FDA-approved and that it does not control the policies of advertising platforms, payment processors, acquirers or card brands ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/healthcare/hhs-peptide-compounding-policy-what-healthcare-businesses-need-to-know/)). Separately, Google restricts promotion of services related to online prescribing, dispensation and sale of prescription drugs, and requires advertiser certification ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031)). Lawful operation, product status, certification, channel eligibility and account status are five different questions. FAQ
Q: Does a live competitor ad prove that offer is allowed?
A: No. Ad delivery reflects a platform review decision at a point in time against that platform's current rules, and those rules are independent of third-party certification. LegitScript states it does not control the policies of advertising platforms, payment processors, acquirers or card brands, and that businesses should independently confirm each platform's current requirements and the effective date of any changes ([LegitScript](https://www.legitscript.com/healthcare/hhs-peptide-compounding-policy-what-healthcare-businesses-need-to-know/)).
Q: Can competitor revenue or ad spend be inferred reliably?
A: Not from visible activity. Ad counts, creative volume, estimated traffic and social following are weak proxies shaped by budget pacing, geography, auction dynamics and tooling artifacts. We treat them as directional signals at most, label them as inference, and never convert them into a revenue or spend figure presented as fact.
Q: What sources can be used without account access?
A: Public-facing pages, visible pricing and offer terms, publicly viewable ad transparency listings, published location and staffing information, regulatory and certification lookups, and dated captures of observable material. Each entry records the source, the retrieval date and the specific claim it supports.
Q: How is confidential company data protected?
A: Authorized internal data is used only within a written scope that names the datasets, fields, purpose, access list and duration. Public research is kept separate from proprietary information in the working record, and sensitive analysis can run on owned local infrastructure for HIPAA-aware inference. Legal, clinical and regulatory conclusions require qualified review on your side.
