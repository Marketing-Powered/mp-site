Choose the optimization event before increasing spend
Google Ads uses primary conversion actions for bidding when a campaign uses their associated goal. Conversion configuration therefore deserves executive attention, not just technical implementation. [Google Ads conversion goals](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10995103?hl=en).
Our recommendation: choose the deepest permitted, reliable and sufficiently frequent event available. Do not select admissions automatically when outcomes are sparse, delayed or unsuitable for platform use.
Agree on qualification with admissions first. Consider service fit, geography, duplicate inquiries and whether meaningful contact occurred. A family member seeking appropriate care should not be excluded simply because they are calling for someone else.
When evaluating PPC for addiction treatment centers, require clear answers about:
* Optimization: Which events influence bidding?
* Qualification: What makes an inquiry appropriate?
* Ownership: Who controls the account and reporting access?
* Accountability: Who owns campaign, intake and policy decisions?
* Measurement boundaries: Which outcomes stay internal?
How we approach addiction treatment PPC
### Design around services and locations
Campaign planning starts with the levels of care, locations and services your organization actually offers.
Use keyword-to-call relevance as the standard. The search intent, ad, landing page and intake conversation should describe the same service. Location messaging should not imply that every facility offers every program.
Review landing pages for service clarity, contact options and an understandable next step. Align spending decisions with operating capacity rather than treating every location as interchangeable.
### Review exclusions with context
Search-term review should separate appropriate demand from irrelevant intent.
Job searches and requests for unavailable services warrant exclusion review. Family inquiries require context, not blanket negative keywords.
Our work focuses on documenting exclusion decisions, their rationale and responsibility for review. Budget and targeting changes should be equally traceable.
### Resolve certification and targeting dependencies
For U.S. advertising of recovery-oriented drug and alcohol addiction services, Google requires LegitScript certification and Google certification. Treat these as launch dependencies. [Google healthcare and medicines policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031?hl=en).
Google prohibits advertiser-curated audiences, including Customer Match and advertiser data segments, for sensitive-interest services. Our paid-search approach centers on relevant search intent rather than remarketing lists. [Google restricted targeting policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465?hl=en).
Campaign quality assurance should cover targeting, budgets, conversion settings, policy-reviewed creative and landing-page accuracy. Assign responsibility for reviewing changes after launch, too.
Track admissions without treating every outcome as platform data
Business attribution and ad-platform optimization are different jobs. Leadership needs visibility into downstream acquisition performance even when those outcomes must remain outside the advertising platform.
A proposed internal measurement workflow begins with the paid-search inquiry. Intake then records qualification and, where authorized data supports attribution, connects it to a scheduled assessment or admission. Restricted internal reporting supports acquisition decisions.
Any proposed transfer to an advertising platform requires a separate decision.
Google supports offline conversion imports using click identifiers. That technical capability does not establish permission to upload treatment-related outcomes. [Google offline conversion imports](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2998031?hl=en).
Google prohibits sensitive-category conversion information, including health or medical information, in enhanced conversions and store-sales uploads. We do not use enhanced conversions to upload treatment-admission conversions. [Google customer data policies](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/7475709?hl=en).
Before configuring measurement, establish permitted fields, identifiers, destinations, access and retention with the appropriate privacy and legal advisers. Keep patient records out of routine campaign-review materials.
### Use a scorecard that separates the decisions
Recommended measures include:
* Cost per qualified inquiry: Spend divided by inquiries meeting agreed criteria.
* Inquiry-to-assessment rate: The share of qualified inquiries reaching a scheduled assessment.
* Inquiry-to-admission rate: The share of qualified inquiries reaching admission.
* Cost per attributed admission: Attributable spend divided by attributed admissions.
* Unresolved inquiries: Records requiring reconciliation before drawing conclusions.
Use consistent cohorts and allow outcomes time to mature. Identify unmatched records and incomplete attribution rather than presenting partial reporting as complete.
Document any proposed offline conversion value and its permitted use. Distinguish an estimated value used for decision-making from measured revenue.

FAQ
Q: What is the right optimization event for rehab PPC campaigns?
A: Our recommendation is the deepest permitted event with dependable data and sufficient frequency. Evaluate qualified inquiries as a candidate, not a universal answer.
Q: How should drug rehab PPC be measured against qualified calls or admissions?
A: Report cost per qualified inquiry, inquiry-to-admission rate and cost per attributed admission separately. Agree on qualification, duplicates and reporting cohorts before comparing performance.
Q: Must admissions be uploaded to Google Ads?
A: No. Design internal attribution separately from platform measurement. Admission uploads should not be a prerequisite for accountable acquisition reporting.
Q: What should an agency explain before taking over?
A: The agency should explain its proposed changes, bidding events, policy responsibilities, measurement boundaries and access requirements. Require clarity about dependencies before approving implementation.
