When to hire a rehab marketing agency
Consider outside support when you need to:
* Build demand for a new location or level of care.
* Reconcile marketing inquiries with admissions records.
* Understand why calls do not progress.
* Coordinate acquisition across locations with different capacity.
* Resolve account restrictions before expanding spend.
* Establish clear ownership of accounts, tracking and reporting.
An agency is not always the first fix. If qualified calls go unanswered, address intake coverage before increasing demand. If the clinical team cannot accept additional admissions, set an operating constraint before setting a growth target.
The purpose of an acquisition review is to distinguish demand problems from conversion, capacity and measurement problems.
Diagnose the path, not just the campaign
Use one program and location to trace the acquisition chain:
Search or ad → landing page → inquiry → intake review → assessment → admission
Then examine each handoff:
* Does the search intent match the advertised service?
* Does the page accurately describe the program and location?
* Can people reach intake during advertised hours?
* Are duplicate inquiries separated from new inquiries?
* Are non-progression reasons recorded consistently?
* Can authorized reporting connect the original source to the outcome?
Do not assume the weakest stage is advertising. A campaign change will not repair an unanswered call or a missing admissions disposition.

Acquisition economics by level of care
Start with your program’s own economics, not an industry-wide cost-per-admission target. Set budgets against available capacity, acquisition costs, financial assumptions supplied by your organization and the time required to observe outcomes.
Keep modeled estimates separate from measured results.
### Detox admissions
For detox acquisition, establish how campaigns will reflect bed availability, admission hours and intake coverage. Make the service scope clear before someone calls.
Measure qualified inquiries and completed admissions separately. Record unavailable capacity as an operating constraint, not automatically as poor marketing quality. Clinical eligibility remains the clinical team’s decision.
### Residential treatment marketing
For residential programs, build a clear explanation of the setting, services, admissions process and location. Give intake a consistent way to record follow-up status rather than counting every subsequent contact as new demand.
Evaluate acquisition by location and inquiry cohort so admissions are not casually attributed to the month they occurred.
### PHP, IOP and outpatient
For partial hospitalization, intensive outpatient and outpatient programs, establish the actual service area, attendance expectations, appointment availability and scheduling process.
Distinguish booked assessments from completed assessments and treatment starts. A scheduled appointment is a useful intermediate measure, not evidence of attendance.
* Available beds or appointments; Decision it should inform: Whether to expand, maintain or reduce acquisition
* Service area and program schedule; Decision it should inform: Geography and landing-page information
* Intake coverage; Decision it should inform: Ad scheduling and contact options
* Coverage information confirmed by intake; Decision it should inform: Accurate service information and non-progression reporting
* Clinical acceptance criteria; Decision it should inform: Provider-reviewed descriptions, not agency clinical judgments
Do not use payer information as a substitute for clinical fit. Have your organization’s compliance leadership review proposed qualification rules and financial arrangements.
Define qualified demand with admissions
Lead quality is defined with admissions, not by an ad-platform conversion label.
Use a shared measurement dictionary:
* Inquiry: A recorded contact, with duplicates identified.
* Qualified inquiry: Intake confirms that the request concerns an offered service within the agreed operational scope.
* Benefits verification: Where applicable, the coverage check and its status are recorded separately.
* Assessment: Scheduled and completed assessments remain distinct.
* Admission or treatment start: The provider records entry following its clinical and administrative process.
Do not make insurance verification a universal prerequisite in a report covering different payment pathways.
For rehab call quality, separate service relevance from call handling. An unanswered inquiry, a request for an unoffered service and a clinically declined admission describe different problems.
Use first-party source and intake records only within approved data boundaries. An unknown outcome should remain unknown, not be counted as a failed admission or assigned to a channel without evidence.
What an addiction treatment agency should report monthly
A monthly report should connect spend to qualified inquiries, assessments and admissions by program and location. It should explain intake performance, capacity constraints, attribution limits and the next decisions. Every stage needs a definition, a reporting period and an accountable owner.
The following is a reporting framework, not client performance data.
* Investment; What it should show: Spend by channel, program and location
* Inquiry quality; What it should show: New inquiries, duplicates, qualified inquiries and non-progression reasons
* Intake execution; What it should show: Answered and missed contacts, follow-up status and assessment scheduling
* Outcomes; What it should show: Completed assessments and admissions where authorized records support attribution
* Economics; What it should show: Cost per qualified inquiry and attributable admission, with formulas disclosed
* Operating context; What it should show: Capacity changes, intake coverage and campaign adjustments
* Measurement limits; What it should show: Missing dispositions, unmatched records and attribution assumptions
* Action plan; What it should show: What changes next, why and who owns it
Calculate qualified-inquiry-to-admission yield using the same inquiry cohort and a stated observation window. Do not divide unrelated monthly totals and present the result as a reliable conversion rate.
Governance comes before tracking expansion
### Certification and audience restrictions
For advertising recovery-oriented drug and alcohol addiction services in the United States, Google requires LegitScript certification and Google certification. [Google healthcare advertising policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031?hl=en)
Google also treats addiction counseling as sensitive health content and restricts advertiser-curated audiences for sensitive services. Do not build the acquisition plan around retargeting treatment-site visitors. [Google health policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16701855?hl=en) [Google personalized advertising restrictions](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465)
LegitScript describes certification as supporting eligibility on platforms including Google and Meta, subject to each platform’s policies. Certification is not the same as platform approval. [LegitScript certification FAQ](https://www.legitscript.com/certification/addiction-treatment-certification/faq/)
Review requirements for each proposed channel, geography and service before launch.
### Privacy and data control
Design the initial audit around aggregate or properly de-identified information wherever possible. Removing names alone is not a sufficient de-identification standard; HHS describes Safe Harbor and Expert Determination methods. [HHS de-identification guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification/index.html)
Do not send patient records or call recordings with an initial audit request. Any proposed PHI access should wait for your compliance team’s direction, a documented permitted purpose and signed agreements appropriate to the relationship. HHS requires a business associate agreement when a tracking vendor meets the business associate definition. [HHS tracking technology guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/hipaa-online-tracking/index.html)
Marketing Powered has owned, local AI infrastructure for HIPAA-aware inference. Deployment boundaries still need to be defined. Owned compute does not mean every workload runs locally or replace account ownership, access controls and data-handling agreements.
What to ask before signing
Evaluate any addiction treatment marketing agency against these criteria:
* Measurement: Can it explain what is attributable and what remains unknown?
* Intake collaboration: Who defines qualification and resolves disputed dispositions?
* Capacity: How will operating constraints change investment?
* Governance: Who approves claims, tracking and platform submissions?
* Ownership: What do the contract and account permissions say about accounts, numbers, data exports and transition access?
* Accountability: Who owns paid media, organic search, landing pages and reporting?
Ask for the reporting structure before the proposal’s performance narrative. A useful report should make decisions clearer, including when not to spend more.
What to scope into an acquisition audit
Start with a defined program, location and business question. Agree access, deliverables, timing and commercial terms before work begins.
The proposed review should cover:
* Account structure, permissions and conversion definitions.
* Search intent, service messaging and landing-page continuity.
* Aggregate intake outcomes and missing dispositions.
* Capacity constraints by program and geography.
* Certification status and tracking-review dependencies.
* A prioritized action plan and monthly reporting framework.
For onboarding, identify a marketing owner, admissions lead and compliance contact. Establish a capacity-update process and decision cadence. Begin with the highest-priority supported changes rather than rebuilding every system at once.

FAQ
Q: How should a rehab marketing agency be measured?
A: Use qualified inquiries, completed assessments and attributable admissions by program and location. Include intake performance, capacity and data completeness. Treat cost per lead as an activity measure, not the final assessment.
Q: Do detox, residential and outpatient need separate campaigns?
A: Separate them when their services, locations, budgets or intake pathways require different decisions. At minimum, preserve program-level reporting rather than combining every inquiry into one “rehab” total.
Q: Can admissions data be sent back to advertising platforms?
A: Do not assume permission. Internal attribution and platform conversion uploads are separate decisions. Review the proposed fields, destination, purpose, applicable privacy requirements and platform terms before implementation.
Q: Is this operating model limited to addiction treatment?
A: No. The planning discipline also applies to hospitals, healthcare organizations, professional services and other regulated or multi-location markets. The [behavioral health overview](/who-we-help/behavioral-health) provides the wider specialty context.
