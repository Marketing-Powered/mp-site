Why Specialized Rehab Marketing Matters
Rehab marketing operates under constraints that general healthcare marketing does not face. Google Ads classifies addiction treatment as a sensitive vertical, which means no retargeting, restricted audience targeting, and LegitScript certification requirements before ads can run. Meta places behavioral health content under Special Ad Categories, limiting demographic and interest-based targeting. These restrictions exist for good reason, but they make patient acquisition fundamentally different from marketing a dermatology practice or urgent care clinic.
Most agencies approach substance use marketing the same way they approach any other healthcare vertical: broad targeting, generic messaging, and a hope that volume will compensate for precision. Understanding the Compliance Landscape
Compliance in addiction treatment marketing is not optional, and it's not simple. Three layers of regulation govern what you can say, where you can say it, and who you can say it to.
First, platform policies. Google requires LegitScript certification before running addiction treatment ads. Advertisers must complete a verification process and maintain certification to advertise. Meta restricts targeting options under Special Ad Categories, which limits the precision available in audience building.
Second, federal and state regulations. HIPAA governs how patient information flows through your marketing systems. The HHS Office for Civil Rights has clarified that tracking pixels can create HIPAA liability if they transmit PHI to third parties. State regulations vary, with some states imposing additional restrictions on behavioral health advertising claims.
Third, ethical standards. Organizations like NAATP have established advertising ethics codes that go beyond legal minimums. Making claims about success rates, using patient testimonials without proper consent, or implying guaranteed outcomes can damage your reputation even if they don't trigger regulatory action.
Marketing Powered maintains HIPAA awareness across our infrastructure and builds campaigns with LegitScript requirements in mind. We've managed paid media in this vertical long enough to know where the lines are, and we stay well inside them.
