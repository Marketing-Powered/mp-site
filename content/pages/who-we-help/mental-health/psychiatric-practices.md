Understanding the Unique Needs of Psychiatric Practices
Why generic marketing fails in psychiatry
Psychiatric practices operate in a category where standard marketing playbooks break down. The challenges are distinct: persistent stigma around mental health treatment, intense competition from telehealth platforms and large health systems, and patient acquisition cycles that look nothing like primary care or elective procedures.
Most agencies approach psychiatry marketing the same way they approach dermatology or dentistry. They run broad awareness campaigns, optimize for clicks, and report on impressions without connecting any of it to actual patient volume. That approach burns budget and delivers leads who are not ready for psychiatric care, not qualified for your services, or not located in your service area.
Marketing Powered brings operator-level experience to psychiatric provider marketing. We scaled Haven Health from 3 to 24 locations, managing the marketing infrastructure through every stage of growth. That experience taught us what works in regulated healthcare verticals and what creates compliance risk. We understand that psychiatric practices need marketing that respects the sensitivity of the patient population while still generating measurable business outcomes.
Your practice faces specific constraints: Google Ads sensitive healthcare vertical restrictions that prohibit retargeting, HIPAA requirements that limit how you can communicate with prospects, and platform policies that restrict certain mental health advertising. We build campaigns that work within those boundaries, not around them.

Compliance and Ethical Marketing in Psychiatry
Protecting your practice while growing it
Compliance is not a checkbox in psychiatric marketing. It is a structural requirement that shapes every campaign decision. Marketing Powered operates with LegitScript and HIPAA awareness built into our processes, not bolted on as an afterthought.


Google Ads classifies mental health services as a sensitive healthcare vertical. That designation prohibits retargeting, limits personalization, and requires careful ad copy review. Many agencies either do not know these restrictions exist or try to work around them in ways that create account suspension risk. We build campaigns that comply from day one.
HIPAA considerations extend beyond your EHR. Marketing systems that collect patient information, track website behavior, or integrate with scheduling platforms must handle data appropriately. We design marketing infrastructure with data handling in mind, ensuring that your growth efforts do not create compliance exposure.
Ethical marketing in psychiatry also means honest representation. We do not make outcome claims, promise specific results, or use fear-based messaging. Your marketing should reflect the professionalism of your clinical practice. Patients seeking psychiatric care deserve accurate information about your services, not manipulation.
